Length is a physical quantity that represents the measurement of distance between two points. Wavelength, specifically, refers to the distance between two consecutive points on a wave where the wave pattern repeats itself.

Common units of length include meters (m), centimeters (cm), and kilometers (km). In terms of wavelength, examples of related units include nanometers (nm) for electromagnetic waves and meters (m) for sound waves.

  • Wavelength is crucial in understanding properties of waves in various fields such as physics, chemistry, and engineering.
  • Length is a fundamental quantity used in everyday measurements, construction, and various scientific calculations.
