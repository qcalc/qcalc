Linear density is a measure of mass per unit length along a one-dimensional object. It describes how much mass is distributed along a certain length of an object, such as a wire or a thread.

Common examples of units related to linear density include grams per meter (g/m), kilograms per kilometer (kg/km), and pounds per foot (lb/ft).

  • Linear density is crucial in determining the strength and flexibility of materials
  • It is used in industries like textiles for quality control
  • Helps in calculating the efficiency of cables and wires
  • Important in designing and manufacturing products like ropes and cables.
