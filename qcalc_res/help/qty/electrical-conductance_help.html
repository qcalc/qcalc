Electrical conductance is a measure of how easily an electric current can flow through a material. It is the reciprocal of electrical resistance, indicating the ability of a material to conduct electricity.

Common units of measurement for electrical conductance include Siemens (S), mho (ohm spelled backwards), and reciprocal ohm (1/Ω).

  • Electrical conductance is crucial in determining the efficiency of electrical components.
  • It is essential in designing circuits and systems for optimal performance.
  • Understanding conductance helps in troubleshooting and maintaining electrical systems.
