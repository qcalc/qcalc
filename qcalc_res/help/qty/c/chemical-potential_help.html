Chemical potential is a measure of the energy that can be absorbed or released during a chemical reaction.

Molar energy refers to the amount of energy contained in a specified number of molecules or atoms of a substance. It is a measurement of the internal energy of a system on a per mole basis.

  • Chemical potential and molar energy are crucial in understanding the behavior of substances in chemical reactions.
  • They play a key role in determining the direction of reactions and the stability of compounds.
