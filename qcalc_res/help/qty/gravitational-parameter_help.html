Gravitational Parameter is a physical quantity that represents the combined mass and gravitational force of an object.

This parameter is commonly used in celestial mechanics and orbital dynamics to calculate the trajectories of objects in space. It is measured in units such as cubic meters per second squared (m^3/s^2) or kilometers cubed per second squared (km^3/s^2).

  • Used in calculating orbits of planets, moons, and satellites
  • Essential in spacecraft navigation and mission planning
  • Helps in understanding the gravitational interactions between celestial bodies
