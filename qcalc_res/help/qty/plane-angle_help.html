A plane angle is a measure of rotation between two intersecting lines in a two-dimensional space. It is typically measured in degrees or radians and represents the amount of rotation needed to move from one line to the other.

Some common examples of units of measurement for plane angles include degrees, radians, and gradians. Degrees are commonly used for everyday measurements, while radians are frequently used in mathematical calculations. Gradians, on the other hand, are used in some specialized fields such as surveying and engineering.

  • Plane angles are essential in geometry for measuring shapes and angles.
  • They are used in navigation for determining directions and bearings.
  • In physics, plane angles are used to calculate forces and torques in mechanical systems.
