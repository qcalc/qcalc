Welcome to qCalc

Explore qCalc, featuring a comprehensive array of calculators crafted to simplify intricate computations across diverse disciplines such as business, engineering, health sciences, mathematics, and beyond. Our commitment is to deliver intuitive and reliable tools that empower users to effortlessly tackle calculations with precision and ease.

Mission

qCalc was born from a passion for using technology to simplify calculation. Its mission is to democratize access to sophisticated technical and programming libraries, making them available to the general user. As a free-to-use web app, qCalc provides a diverse range of user-friendly tools. Although it refers to these tools as 'calculators', they offer not only trivial and simple calculations but also advanced computations, artificial intelligence-based solutions, and simulations.

What It Offers

qCalc has a diverse collection of calculators, each designed to address specific needs. Whether you're a common person seeking assistance in finance, a health-conscious individual tracking fitness metrics, a photographer trying to improve photographing techniques using calculations, a professional needing specialized computations for your business, an engineer requiring precise measurements, or a student exploring academic challenges, qCalc stands as your trusted resource. You can also create your own calculators to simplify your everyday calculations and unlock new insights effortlessly.

Values

qCalc believes in:

  1. Accuracy: Ensuring calculators provide reliable and precise results.
  2. Usability: Designing intuitive and easy-to-navigate interfaces for all users.
  3. Continuous Improvement: Regularly updating and expanding toolset to meet the evolving needs of the users.
  4. Customer Satisfaction: Prioritizing user feedback and striving to provide the best possible experience.
