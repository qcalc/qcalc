Solid angle is a measure of how much of a three-dimensional space is taken up by an object as seen from a specific point. It is analogous to the two-dimensional concept of angle, but in three dimensions.

Common units used to measure solid angle include steradians (SI unit) and square degrees. These units are used in various fields such as astronomy, physics, and engineering to quantify the amount of space an object or light source occupies in three dimensions.

  • Used in optics to measure the intensity of light sources
  • Important in calculating radiation patterns of antennas
  • Essential in determining the field of view of cameras and sensors.
