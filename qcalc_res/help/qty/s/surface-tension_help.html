Surface tension is a property of liquids that causes the surface to behave like a thin elastic membrane. It is measured in units of force per unit length.

Common units of measurement for surface tension include Newtons per meter (N/m) and dynes per centimeter (dyn/cm).

  • Surface tension is important in understanding the behavior of liquids at interfaces.
  • It plays a crucial role in phenomena such as capillary action and droplet formation.

Spectral irradiance (fr) is a measure of the radiant flux received per unit area per unit wavelength. It is commonly used in fields such as astronomy and environmental science.

Common units of measurement for spectral irradiance include Watts per square meter per nanometer (W/m^2/nm) and Watts per square centimeter per angstrom (W/cm^2/Å).

  • Spectral irradiance is crucial in determining the amount of energy received from different parts of the electromagnetic spectrum.
  • It is used in various applications such as measuring solar radiation and characterizing light sources.
