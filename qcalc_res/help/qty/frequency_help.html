Frequency, Heart Rate refers to the number of times the heart beats per minute. It is a vital indicator of cardiovascular health and overall fitness level.

Common units of measurement for Heart Rate include beats per minute (bpm) and pulses per minute (ppm).

  • Monitoring heart rate during exercise helps to ensure safe and effective workouts
  • Abnormal heart rates can be a sign of various health conditions
  • Tracking heart rate over time can provide valuable insights into heart health and fitness progress
