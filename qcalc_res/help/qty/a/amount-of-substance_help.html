Amount of substance is a measure of the quantity of particles in a sample, typically expressed in moles. It represents the number of atoms, ions, molecules, or other entities in a substance.

Common examples of units of measurement for amount of substance include mole, kilomole, millimole, and micromole. These units are used in chemistry to quantify the number of particles in a given sample, allowing for accurate measurements and calculations.

  • Used in stoichiometry to determine the amount of reactants and products in a chemical reaction
  • Essential for measuring concentrations of solutes in solutions
  • Plays a crucial role in determining the properties and behavior of substances in various processes and reactions.
