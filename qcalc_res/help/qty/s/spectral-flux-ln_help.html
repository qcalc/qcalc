Spectral flux (ln) is a measure of the flow of energy per unit frequency range in a given spectrum. It quantifies the rate at which energy is transmitted through different frequency components of a signal, providing valuable information about the distribution of energy across the spectrum.

Common examples of related units of measurements include spectral power, spectral density, and spectral energy. These measurements are essential in various fields such as signal processing, audio engineering, and astronomy for analyzing and characterizing signals in the frequency domain.

  • Spectral flux (ln) helps in analyzing the energy distribution in signals
  • It is useful in identifying key frequency components in a spectrum
  • Enables comparison of different signals based on their spectral characteristics
