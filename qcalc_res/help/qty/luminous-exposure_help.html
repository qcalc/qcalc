Luminous exposure is a measure of the total amount of light energy that falls on a given surface over a period of time. It takes into account both the intensity of the light and the duration for which the surface is exposed to that light.

Common examples of related units of measurements include lux-seconds, lumen-seconds, and candela-seconds. These units are used in various fields such as photography, lighting design, and photobiology to quantify the amount of light received by a surface or an object.

  • Luminous exposure is crucial in determining the effectiveness of lighting systems in different applications.
  • It is used in evaluating the potential impact of light exposure on living organisms.
  • In photography, luminous exposure helps in capturing the right amount of light for a well-exposed image.
