Electric current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor, measured in amperes. It is the rate at which electric charge passes through a specific point in a circuit.

Common units of measurements related to electric current include amperes (A), milliamperes (mA), and kiloamperes (kA).

  • Electric current is essential for the operation of electrical devices.
  • It is used in various applications such as powering electronic devices, lighting, and heating systems.
  • Understanding and controlling electric current is crucial for electrical safety and efficiency.
