Electric charge is a fundamental property of matter that determines how objects interact with each other through electromagnetic forces. It is a property of particles that can be positive or negative, and is measured in units called coulombs.

Some common examples of related units of measurements include: - Coulombs (C) - Milliampere-hours (mAh) - Elementary charge (e)

Application and Importance: - Electric charge is essential for understanding how electricity works in circuits. - It is crucial for designing electronic devices and power systems. - Electric charge plays a key role in fields such as physics, engineering, and technology.
