# Calculate Age from Date of Birth

## Purpose

This calculator shows the elapsed time from a date of birth to today as years,
months, and days.

## Input

### Date of Birth

Enter the date of birth. A date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format, such as `1999-12-31`,
is a clear choice.

## Result

The result is the time elapsed between the entered date and the current date,
displayed in years, months, and days.

## Interpretation

The calculation uses the date on which you run the calculator, so the result
changes as time passes. It is an elapsed-duration calculation and may not meet
the specific age-definition rules used for legal, administrative, or other
formal purposes.
