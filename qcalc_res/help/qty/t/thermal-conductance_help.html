Thermal conductance is a physical quantity that represents the ability of a material to conduct heat. It is a measure of how easily heat can flow through a material.

Common examples of related units of measurements include thermal conductivity, which measures the ability of a material to conduct heat, and thermal resistance, which measures a material's resistance to heat flow.

  • Thermal conductance is crucial in determining the efficiency of heat transfer in various systems.
  • It is used in the design of insulation materials to improve energy efficiency.
  • Understanding thermal conductance is essential in the development of heating and cooling systems for buildings and electronics.
