Dynamic fluid viscosity refers to the measure of a fluid's resistance to flow under applied shear stress. It is a key property that characterizes how easily a fluid deforms and moves when subjected to external forces.

Common units used to measure dynamic fluid viscosity include Pascal-seconds (Pa·s), Poise (P), and centipoise (cP). These units are essential in various fields such as fluid dynamics, chemical engineering, and materials science for understanding and predicting the behavior of fluids in different applications.

  • Dynamic fluid viscosity is crucial in designing efficient pipelines and pumping systems.
  • It plays a critical role in the manufacturing of products such as paints, adhesives, and lubricants.
  • Understanding dynamic fluid viscosity is essential in optimizing processes in industries like food and beverage, pharmaceuticals, and cosmetics.
