Mass flow rate is the measure of the amount of mass passing through a given area per unit of time. It is commonly used in fluid dynamics to quantify the rate at which a fluid is flowing through a pipe or channel.

Common units of measurement related to mass flow rate include kilograms per second, grams per minute, and pounds per hour. These units are used in various industries such as manufacturing, chemical processing, and transportation to monitor and control the flow of materials.

  • Mass flow rate is crucial for ensuring efficient operation of industrial processes.
  • It is used in designing and optimizing systems that involve fluid flow.
  • Accurate measurement of mass flow rate is essential for maintaining product quality and consistency.
