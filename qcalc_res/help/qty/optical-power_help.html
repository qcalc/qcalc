Optical power is a measure of the ability of a lens or optical system to converge or diverge light rays. Wavenumber and wavevector are both measures of the spatial frequency of a wave, with wavenumber being used in the context of wave propagation in a medium and wavevector being used in the context of wave phenomena in an abstract vector space.

Common units of measurement for optical power include diopters, for wavenumber include reciprocal meters (m^-1), and for wavevector include radians per meter (rad/m).

  • Optical power is crucial in designing lenses for vision correction
  • Wavenumber is important in studying wave propagation in different mediums
  • Wavevector is essential in analyzing wave phenomena in vector spaces
