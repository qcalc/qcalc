An electric dipole moment is a measure of the separation of positive and negative charges within a system.

Common examples of related units of measurements include Debye (D), Coulomb meter (Cm), and atomic units (au).

  • Electric dipole moments are crucial in understanding molecular structures.
  • They play a significant role in fields such as chemistry, physics, and engineering.
  • Electric dipole moments are utilized in studying polar molecules and their interactions.
