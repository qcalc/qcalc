Electric potential is a scalar quantity that represents the amount of electric potential energy per unit charge at a specific point in an electric field.

Common examples of related units of measurements include volt (V), which is the SI unit for electric potential, as well as kilovolt (kV) and millivolt (mV) which are commonly used in various electrical systems.

  • Electric potential helps in understanding the behavior of charged particles in an electric field.
  • It is crucial in determining the flow of current in circuits.
  • Electric potential plays a key role in the design and operation of electrical devices and systems.
