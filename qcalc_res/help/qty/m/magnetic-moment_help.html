Magnetic moment, also known as magnetic dipole moment, refers to the measure of the strength and orientation of a magnetic source.

Common examples of related units of measurements include the Ampere square meter (A m²) and the Bohr magneton (μB).

  • Magnetic moments are crucial in understanding the behavior of magnetic materials.
  • They play a key role in various scientific fields such as physics, chemistry, and engineering.
  • Magnetic moments are used in applications like MRI machines, magnetic data storage, and magnetic resonance spectroscopy.
