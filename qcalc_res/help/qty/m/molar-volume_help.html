Molar volume refers to the amount of space occupied by one mole of a substance.

Common units of measurement related to molar volume include liters per mole (L/mol) and cubic meters per mole (m^3/mol).

  • Molar volume is a crucial concept in chemistry, especially in determining the properties of gases.
  • It helps in calculating the volume of gases at different conditions of temperature and pressure.
  • Molar volume also plays a significant role in stoichiometry, aiding in the calculation of reactants and products in chemical reactions.
