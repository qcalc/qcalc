Centrifugal force is a fictitious force that appears to act on an object moving in a circular path, pushing it away from the center of rotation.

Some common examples of related units of measurements include centripetal force, angular velocity, and radius of rotation.

  • Centrifugal force is crucial in understanding the behavior of objects in circular motion.
  • It is important in various fields such as engineering, physics, and mechanics for designing and analyzing rotating systems.
