# Body Mass Index (BMI)

## Purpose

The **Body Mass Index (BMI)** calculator determines BMI from body weight and height. BMI can be used as a screening measure to assess weight categories based on height.

## Use Cases

* Check whether your weight falls within a commonly used BMI range.
* Monitor changes in BMI over time.

## How to Use

1. Enter your **weight** and **height**.
2. Click **Calculate** to calculate your BMI.

## Input Values

* **Weight** — Body weight in `lb`, `kg`, etc.
* **Height** — Body height in `m`, `cm`, `ft`, etc.

Other compatible units may also be entered.

## Output Values

* **BMI** — Body Mass Index, expressed in **kg/m²**.

### BMI Categories

| BMI           | Category      |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| Below 18.5    | Underweight   |
| 18.5–24.9     | Normal weight |
| 25.0–29.9     | Overweight    |
| 30.0 or above | Obesity       |

> BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat or diagnose an individual's health condition.
