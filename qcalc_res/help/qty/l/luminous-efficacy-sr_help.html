Luminous efficacy (sr) is a measure of how efficiently a light source produces visible light. It describes the amount of light emitted per unit of power input, taking into account the sensitivity of the human eye to different wavelengths of light.

Common related units of measurement include lumens per watt (lm/W) and lux per watt (lx/W), which are used to quantify the efficiency of lighting systems. Luminous efficacy is an important parameter in designing energy-efficient lighting solutions and assessing the effectiveness of different light sources.

  • Luminous efficacy helps in comparing the efficiency of different light sources.
  • It is crucial in designing sustainable lighting systems.
  • The measurement aids in optimizing energy consumption in lighting applications.
