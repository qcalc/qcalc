Spectral irradiance (ln) refers to the measurement of radiant flux per unit area per unit wavelength. It is a crucial parameter in analyzing the distribution of electromagnetic radiation across different wavelengths.

Common examples of related units of measurements include watts per square meter per nanometer (W/m²/nm) or watts per square meter per micrometer (W/m²/μm).

  • Spectral irradiance is used in the study of solar energy and its impact on photovoltaic systems.
  • It helps in understanding the behavior of different light sources and their effect on materials.
  • Spectral irradiance is vital in fields such as astronomy, environmental monitoring, and optical communications.
