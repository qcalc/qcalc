Magnetic field strength refers to the intensity of a magnetic field at a specific point in space. It is a vector quantity that determines the force experienced by a moving charge or magnetic material within the field.

Common units used to measure magnetic field strength include tesla (T) and gauss (G). Magnetization, on the other hand, is a measure of the extent to which a material can be magnetized. It is the alignment of magnetic moments within a material in response to an external magnetic field.

  • Used in designing magnetic materials for various applications
  • Helps in understanding the behavior of magnetic materials
  • Essential in the development of technologies like MRI machines and magnetic storage devices
