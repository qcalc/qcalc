Area is a measure of the amount of space enclosed within a boundary. It is a two-dimensional quantity that represents the size of a surface or shape.

Common examples of units used to measure area include square meters, square feet, acres, and hectares.

  • Area is a fundamental concept in geometry and is used in various fields such as architecture, engineering, and physics.
  • It helps in determining the size of a piece of land, the amount of material needed for construction, and the capacity of a space.
  • Calculating area is essential for designing buildings, planning cities, and understanding spatial relationships.
