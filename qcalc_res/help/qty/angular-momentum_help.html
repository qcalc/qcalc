Angular momentum is a measure of an object's rotational motion. It describes the tendency of an object to keep spinning at a constant rate.

Units related to angular momentum include kilogram meters squared per second (kg m^2/s), Newton meters per second (N m/s), and joule seconds (J s).

  • Angular momentum is crucial in understanding the movement of celestial bodies.
  • It plays a key role in the stability of rotating objects like gyros and tops.
