Fuel economy refers to the measurement of how efficiently a vehicle uses fuel to travel a certain distance. It is typically expressed as the number of miles or kilometers a vehicle can travel per gallon or liter of fuel consumed.

Common examples of units related to fuel economy include miles per gallon (mpg), kilometers per liter (km/l), and liters per 100 kilometers (l/100km).

  • Application and importance:
  • Helps consumers compare the efficiency of different vehicles.
  • Affects the cost of transportation for individuals and businesses.
  • Can contribute to reducing greenhouse gas emissions and air pollution.
  • Influences government regulations on vehicle emissions and fuel efficiency.
